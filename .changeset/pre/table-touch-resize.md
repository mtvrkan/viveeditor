On touch screens, tapping a table cell shows grips on its right and bottom edges; dragging them with a finger or pen sets the column width and the row height in one undoable step. Mice keep the hover handles, the grips stay hidden while typing or scrolling, in read-only mode and with `resizable: false`, and page scrolling outside the grips is unchanged.
