The "More tools" menu is sorted into categories named after the toolbar groups (Text, Format, Lists and quotes, Insert, Paragraph, Links and search, Document). A category with three or more hidden tools opens as a submenu; one or two hidden tools are listed directly under the category name. Tools that own a menu, such as line spacing, alignment, text style, font and File, are a single row that opens their own submenu with the current choice checked, instead of spilling their items into the list without a label. On touch screens and where there is no room beside the menu, a submenu replaces the list and offers a back row; on desktop it opens beside the menu.
